\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

% SOURCE-EN: 288
This paper presents \sys{}, a GPU--LPDDR5X-PIM system that overlaps VLM execution on the GPU with AE computation in PIM for edge VLA inference. Rank-level partitioning keeps every channel available to the GPU, the DQ-aware dataflow reduces the AE's DQ traffic, and the input-preloading microarchitecture consolidates the PIM transfers into contiguous GPU access windows. Overall, \sys{} achieves a $1.82\times$ speedup and a $2.52\times$ energy reduction over the serial GPU baseline. As embodied intelligence models scale up, their memory-bound components will further strain edge memory bandwidth, making heterogeneous GPU--PIM systems such as \sys{} increasingly essential.
